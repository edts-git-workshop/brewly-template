# PRD — Brewly v1.0

**Status:** Draft
**Owner:** Brewly product team

## 1. Problem

Coffee lovers in Indonesia want fresh, specialty beans from local roasters,
but buying them means visiting cafés, following dozens of Instagram accounts,
or ordering one bag at a time with separate shipping fees.

## 2. Goals

- Make it effortless to receive fresh beans every month.
- Give small local roasters a predictable stream of orders.
- Reach 1,000 paying subscribers within 3 months of launch.
